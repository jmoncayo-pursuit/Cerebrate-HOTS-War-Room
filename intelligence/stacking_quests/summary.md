
# Tactical Intelligence: Stacking Power Spikes
Analysis of stacking quests for recently played heroes and their impact on game state.

## 🧵 Stitches: Hungry for More
- **Activation**: Passive (Level 1 Talent)
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Regen Globes.
- **Incremental Gain**: +30 Max HP per globe.
- **Impact at 30+ Globes**: Roughly +900 HP. Combined with his baseline health, this transforms Stitches into a "Raid Boss" who can face-tank structures and survive focus-fire that would melt other tanks. 
- **Strategic Note**: Essential for "Gorge" plays where Stitches must survive the walk back into friendly territory.

## ☄️ Azmodan: Wrath / Gluttony
- **Activation**: Passive (Trait-bound Quest)
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Hits with Globe of Annihilation (Q) on Heroes or killing minions with Q.
- **Max Stacks**: 400.
- **Impact**: Each stack adds to the flat damage of Globe of Annihilation. At 400 stacks, Azmodan can delete backline squishies from half a screen away.
- **Strategic Note**: Missing stacks early is a "Neural Desync". You must consistently hit the wave and heroes simultaneously to hit the 200/400 power spikes before the level 20 objective.

## 🧘 Kharazim: Insight
- **Activation**: level 1 Talent.
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Every 3rd basic attack against *any* target.
- **Reward (100 Stacks)**: Every 3rd hit reduces Basic Ability cooldowns by 1.75 seconds.
- **Impact**: Provides infinite mana sustain and near-constant uptime on Breath of Heaven (Heal) and Deadly Reach. 
- **Strategic Note**: You are a weak solo-laner until 100 stacks. After 100, you become a high-sustain duelist who can solo clear mercenary camps effortlessly.
 
## 🔫 Raynor: Veteran Marksman
- **Activation**: Level 1 Quest.
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Hit heroes with the 4th basic attack (Give 'Em Some Pepper).
- **Impact**: Permanent basic attack damage scaling.
- **Strategic Note**: Focus on "safe" poke during lane phases to build the foundation for your late-game hyper-carry status.

## ❄️ Jaina: Frostbite (Trait Quest)
- **Activation**: Passive Trait.
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Deal damage to Chilled heroes.
- **Standard Reward (12,000 Damage)**: Unlocks *Improved Ice Block* (2.5s Invulnerability).
- **Mythic Reward (30,000 Damage)**: Upgrades to *Ice Cold*. Jaina can move and cast any ability while Ice Block is active.
- **Strategic Note**: This transforms Jaina from a fragile caster into a 2.5s "Invulnerable Tank" who can unleash a full rotation while being completely safe from counter-dive. Speed-running the 30k milestone is now the primary tactical objective.

## 🔨 Muradin: Baseline Quest & Sledgehammer
- **Activation**: Baseline Quest (New).
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Basic Attacks against Heroes who are Slowed, Stunned, or Rooted.
- **Reward**: Grants Storm Bolt (Q) *Pierce* (hits 2 targets) and reduces Storm Bolt cooldown by 1s.
- **Talent Shift**: *Perfect Storm* has been moved to Level 7 (Reset CD on kill). Level 1 is now focused on pure CC-synergy and "Sledgehammer" (500% non-hero damage).
- **Strategic Note**: You are now incentivized to "weave" AAs between Storm Bolt and Thunder Clap stuns to hit the pierce/CD spike. 60-70 stacks is the typical late-game threshold observed.

## 🍗 The Butcher: Fresh Meat (Critical Threat)
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Meat drops from minion (1) and hero (20) kills.
- **Reward (200 Meat)**: +125 Basic Attack damage and +25% Attack Speed.
- **Strategic Note**: You are literally "half a hero" until 200 stacks. Dieing loses meat, delaying your "Online" status. Play for safety until the 200-meat mark.

## 🍄 Nazeebo: Voodoo Ritual
- **Stacking Mechanic**: Nearby minion deaths while poisoned.
- **Growth**: +4 HP and +1 Mana permanently per stack.
- **Level 20 Spike**: *Vile Infection* requires 175 stacks to activate, dealing massive percent-based damage.
- **Strategic Note**: If you are under 100 stacks at level 10, your macro-efficiency is failing. Focus on double-soaking lanes.
